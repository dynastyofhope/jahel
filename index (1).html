Who We Are

Josephine Adidi Humanitarian and Empowerment Legacy Foundation (JAHEL) is a non-profit organization committed to transforming lives and building stronger communities through compassionate action and sustainable development initiatives.

Our Foundation's Story

Founded in memory of Josephine Adidi, a woman whose life was dedicated to service and compassion, JAHEL Foundation carries forward her legacy of hope and empowerment. We believe that every individual has the right to dignity, opportunity, and a better future.

Through our various programs and initiatives, we reach out to the most vulnerable members of our society, providing them with the resources, skills, and support they need to thrive. Our work spans across education, healthcare, economic empowerment, and community development.

With a team of dedicated professionals and volunteers, we are committed to creating sustainable solutions that address the root causes of poverty and inequality.

Our Mission

To inspire hope and empower individuals and communities through humanitarian services, education, and sustainable development programs that create lasting positive change.

Our Vision

To be a leading force in humanitarian action and community empowerment, creating a world where every person has the opportunity to live a dignified and fulfilling life.
